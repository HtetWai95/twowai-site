About Me

Precision Meets
Digital Craft

I'm a BIM Specialist based in Singapore with over 5 years of experience delivering complex façade and infrastructure projects. Currently at Lindner Facades Asia, I work at the intersection of architecture, engineering, and technology.

My expertise spans the full BIM delivery lifecycle — from developing parametric façade models in Revit and Rhino to automating workflows through Dynamo and Grasshopper scripting. I thrive on turning complex design challenges into precise, buildable digital models.

I hold a Specialist Diploma in BIM Management from Singapore Polytechnic and a Bachelor of Civil Engineering, bringing both theoretical rigour and hands-on site experience to every project.
